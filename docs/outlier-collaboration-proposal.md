## Suggested first step

Potential areas for discussion:

- subject-expert participation in a mutually agreed pilot;

Any collaboration is subject to mutual agreement, access and rights review, appropriate safeguards, and approval by all relevant parties. This is a proposal only; no Outlier or Terminal-Bench partnership, endorsement, access, or commitment is implied.
